# Design pillars — L'Ordre Caché

## The promise
The player is not told the world's rules. The world is a system that can be questioned. A small, readable reaction is more valuable than a long exposition.

## Core loop
1. Read the physical situation.
2. Choose a sign (Sun, Rain, Moon, Wind).
3. Apply it and observe the result.
4. Record only a law demonstrated by the experiment.
5. Reuse the law in a context that demands sequencing or transfer.

## Non-goals for this slice
No combat, inventory grind, timers, lives, ads, network dependency, or forced hints. The visual treatment is atmospheric and restrained so the puzzle logic stays legible on a phone.

## Expansion direction
- Region II — Inverted Galleries: gravity, reflection, and spatial transformations.
- Region III — Living Archives: symbols change based on the order of observations.
- Region IV — Heart of Laws: earlier principles combine into non-obvious deductions.

These are future directions, not yet implemented content.
